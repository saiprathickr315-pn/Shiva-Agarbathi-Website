# Next steps (things code alone can't do)

1. **Upload** these files over your current site, then in Google Search Console open *Sitemaps* and resubmit `https://shivaagarbathi.com/sitemap.xml`. Use *URL Inspection → Request indexing* for the home page and each machine page.
2. **Google Business Profile**: create/verify a listing for "Shri Shiva Agarbathi Works" (Bengaluru) with your phone, address, photos and machine categories. This is the biggest factor for "machine manufacturer near me" searches.
3. **Compress images**: the `img2025...jpg` photos are 2–6 MB each. Convert to WebP at ~1600px wide (under 200 KB) with Squoosh or TinyPNG. Also shrink `logo1.png` (600 KB) to ~30 KB. Slow pages rank lower.
4. **Host product images yourself**: several product pages load images from IndiaMART / Amazon / other sites. Download them (or use your own photos) so they can't disappear and so Google credits your site.
5. **Add your full street address** and opening hours to the contact page, then add them to the LocalBusiness schema in `index.html`.
6. **Backlinks**: link to your site from your IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial and YouTube profiles, and ask customers for Google reviews.
7. **Content**: write short pages/blogs such as "How to start an agarbatti business", "Agarbatti machine price in India", "Dhoop vs agarbatti machine". These bring in buyers who search before they're ready to buy.
8. **machine.html** builds its text with JavaScript, so Google can't read it well. It is set to noindex; the individual machine pages carry the ranking instead.
