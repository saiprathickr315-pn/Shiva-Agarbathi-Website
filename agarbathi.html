Automatic and semi-automatic agarbatti making machines

An automatic agarbatti making machine, also called an incense stick making machine, coats bamboo sticks with masala, charcoal or wood-powder paste at a steady speed. Semi-automatic machines need more manpower per kilogram, while a fully automatic agarbatti machine keeps labour low and output consistent. Pick by your daily target, stick length and budget.
